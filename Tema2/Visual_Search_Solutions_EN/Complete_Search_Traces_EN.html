How to read a trace

The first entry is always popped in ascending priority order, with ties broken by creation id. Goals are tested when popped. Repeated states may remain in the frontier and are checked when popped, following Unit2's unified implementation. This is not the specific BFS implementation in AIMA Figure 3.11, which tests goals when generating successors.

The root has depth 0. In DLS, f=−d is the priority, not a negative depth; goals exactly at the limit are accepted. EXPAND generates successors; CUT discards an arrival; LIMIT does not expand because of depth; REOPEN uses a better arrival; GOAL terminates.

The first entry in each frontier is highlighted. #id identifies a node, not a state. Expansion counts exclude the goal. In BFS/DLS, the value stored in closed is d; in UCS/A* it is g. Greedy retains g as trace data, but its priority is h and it does not reopen on g.

The Romania traces retain Unit2's duplicates, so their frontiers may have more entries than hw1soln's compact table. Paths, distances, heuristics and selection rules are preserved; this implementation difference is stated explicitly here.
